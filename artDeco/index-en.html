English Art Deco Burr Walnut Extending Table

Dining / boardroom table · circa 1930–1935 · possibly London
A substantial and unusually sculptural English Art Deco extending table, believed to date from circa 1930–1935 and possibly made in London.

Description

The table is constructed in burr walnut veneer with contrasting walnut accents. The twin pedestals consist of tall rectangular structures wrapped in closely spaced, rounded horizontal ribs, creating a corrugated architectural effect. The pedestals terminate in broad, stepped, and shaped feet, also veneered in figured burr walnut.

The tabletop measures 200 × 100 cm and extends to 300 × 100 cm with two 50 cm extensions. The surface accommodates a removable black glass panel. The wooden surface beneath the glass is plain.

The underside shows traditional timber construction and wooden components associated with the extension mechanism. No maker's label, stamp, or signature has been found, except for four cabinetmaker's assembly marks (numbered I to IV).

The table is located in the Liège region of Belgium.

The castors visible in the photographs are modern transport dollies and are not part of the table.
